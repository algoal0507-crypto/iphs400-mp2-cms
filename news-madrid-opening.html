La Once Mil arrives in Madrid

Published 2026-10-13

The official announcement of September 14, 2026 gave September 22, 2026 as the opening date of La Once Mil in Madrid, at Plaza de las Salesas 11, in collaboration with Los 33, its local partner.

  • Opening date: September 22, 2026
  • Address: Plaza de las Salesas 11, Madrid
  • With: Los 33
  • Hours announced: Tuesday to Saturday, 1:00 pm to 1:00 am; Sunday, 1:00 pm to 7:00 pm. Dine-in only, no takeaway.
  • Announced: September 14, 2026
  • Source: Official announcement (press release, in Spanish)
