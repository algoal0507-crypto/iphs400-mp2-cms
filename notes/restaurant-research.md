# Restaurant research: La Once Mil (Mexico City)

Research conducted 2026-09-29 using `/browse` (gstack) and WebSearch. All facts below are
sourced; anything not directly attributable to a source is marked uncertain or proposed.

## ⚠️ Naming correction — read this first

The client brief this project started from said **"Tacos La Oncemil."** The restaurant's
actual public branding is **"La Once Mil"** (Spanish for "the eleven thousand," a reference
to the 11000 postal code of its original neighborhood). Its Instagram handle is
`@tacoslaoncemil` and its Linktree profile title reads "Taqueria La Oncemil" — so the
"Tacos"/"Taqueria La Oncemil" phrasing does appear in secondary branding, but "La Once Mil"
is the name used in the Michelin Guide, Wikipedia, and its own Instagram display name.
**Recommendation:** use "La Once Mil" as the primary name, "Taqueria La Once Mil" as a
secondary descriptor if needed. Confirm which you prefer before we lock this into `CONTEXT.md`.

**Decided (2026-09-28, in `/grill-with-docs`):** site uses **"La Once Mil"** as the
display name, matching the restaurant's own verified branding. The earlier "Tacos La
Oncemil" spelling in the original client brief was not an intentional name choice —
it's corrected throughout the project going forward.

## ⚠️ Second major correction — there are TWO Mexico City locations, not one

The brief assumed a single CDMX location. In fact there are two, both under the same brand
and the same Instagram account:

1. **Lomas de Chapultepec** (the original, opened 2024) — **Monte Everest 780, Lomas de
   Chapultepec, Miguel Hidalgo, CP 11000, Mexico City.** This is the location that holds the
   Michelin star.
2. **Roma Norte** (a second branch) — **Orizaba 83, Roma Norte, Mexico City.** Per Wikipedia,
   opened later; no evidence it individually holds a Michelin star (the Guide's award and
   listing page are specifically for the Lomas de Chapultepec address).

**Decided (2026-09-29):** this project represents the **Lomas de Chapultepec flagship only**
(Monte Everest 780 — the Michelin-starred location). Roma Norte and Madrid are out of scope;
their details must never appear on this site.

## Uncertain / not independently verified — do not treat as fact

- **Exact current menu prices in pesos.** No official printed or posted menu with MXN prices
  was found. The only price point found is the ~US $20 wagyu taco figure from the Michelin
  magazine piece — likely a journalist's approximate USD conversion, not a menu price. **Do
  not put specific peso prices on the site without a screenshot of an actual menu or receipt
  you (the user) can provide.**
- **Whether the Roma Norte location shares the exact same menu/hours as Lomas de
  Chapultepec.** Not confirmed either way.
- **Hours discrepancy:** a third-party aggregator (novacircle.com, unofficial, not cited
  further here) lists different hours (closed Mondays, 12:30 PM starts) than the Michelin
  Guide page (open Mondays, 12:00 PM starts). Michelin is the more authoritative source and
  is what's recorded above, but restaurant hours can change — recommend confirming via the
  Instagram bio or a direct message before publishing as fact.
- **"67th restaurant" / Grupo Carolo / Blanco Colima connection.** A NYC food newsletter
  (peopleandfood.com, formerly at robmartinez.substack.com) quotes César de la Parra jokingly
  claiming La Once Mil is his "67th restaurant" and identifies him as Executive Chef of Grupo
  Carolo (Blanco Colima). This reads as an offhand joke in an interview, not a verified
  biographical fact, and is unrelated to the CDMX taquerias directly — **excluded from the
  brief** unless you want it as color commentary, clearly labeled as reported speech.
- **laoncemil.com as the "real" official website.** It's linked from the Facebook About
  section, so it is very likely genuine, but it currently has no content to source anything
  from.
